# Suraksha Setu — bank-first pilot plan

## Positioning

A plain-language, multilingual scam-awareness and safe-next-step companion. It complements bank fraud reporting and customer support; it does not replace bank controls, fraud detection, or official reporting.

## First buyer hypothesis

Start with a bank fraud-awareness/customer-education team or customer-experience team. Security, privacy/legal, procurement, accessibility, and risk teams are required reviewers. Do not use a bank name or logo or imply endorsement without written permission.

## Discovery questions

- Which scam categories create the most customer confusion or support demand?
- Which languages and accessibility needs matter most?
- Which official links and support channels should be shown?
- Can the bank sponsor a privacy-preserving pilot without receiving user message content?
- What security assessment, data residency, retention, procurement, and incident-reporting requirements apply?
- What evidence is needed to approve a limited pilot?

## Pilot scope

- Current local-only PWA with English, Tamil, and Hindi language choices.
- Message-pattern and URL-text checks, QR/UPI safety guidance, learning exercises, and official emergency guidance.
- No bank login, account linking, transaction access, SMS/call monitoring, or automated complaint filing.
- No AI until the bank and project owner approve budget, data handling, testing, and safety controls.

## Commercial validation

1. Speak with 5–8 relevant bank stakeholders and 2–3 adjacent partners (NGO, school, or community digital-literacy group) before building enterprise-only features.
2. Offer a time-boxed pilot with written scope, support expectations, success criteria, and no claim of guaranteed fraud prevention.
3. Measure comprehension and time-to-guidance; do not collect underlying suspicious messages for analytics.
4. Discuss pricing after a buyer confirms the problem and procurement path. Do not assume a bank will pay before a signed pilot or purchase order.

## Financial guardrails

- Current approved spend: ₹0/month. Use the existing static site and local tests.
- Profit goal: at least 5% operating margin once paid revenue begins.
- AI usage goal: keep API spend at or below 10% of collected revenue. At zero revenue, the only compliant AI spend is ₹0.
- Before enabling AI, establish per-request limits, project spend limits, a kill switch, and monthly usage review. Check current provider and hosting pricing when approval is requested.
