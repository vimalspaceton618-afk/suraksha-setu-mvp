# Security policy

## Product commitments

1. No deceptive safe/unsafe certainty; findings are warnings, not proof.
2. No AI or external intelligence requests in this release.
3. Never transmit checked text, URLs, identifiers, or sensitive user information.
4. Do not ask for phone, SMS, contact, microphone, camera, location, accessibility, or screen-capture permissions in the PWA.
5. Do not silently monitor users or other apps.
6. Do not imitate government/bank branding or claim official affiliation.
7. Do not claim to freeze funds, file reports, trace criminals, or guarantee recovery.

## Reporting a vulnerability

This prototype has no public security-response team or production endpoint. Before a public launch, publish a monitored security contact, define response timelines, and document coordinated vulnerability disclosure. Do not collect real victim evidence in test deployments.

## Test safely

Use fictional sample messages and dummy URLs only. Never enter real OTPs, PINs, passwords, banking details, or victim data into a demo deployment.
